Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy and explaining clearly how your data is collected, used, and safeguarded.

Last Updated: July 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Suki App ("Company," "we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information Provided by You

When you create an account or use our services, we collect the following personal information:

  • Account Registration: Username, email address, password, first name, last name, and contact number
  • User Profile: Additional profile information you choose to provide
  • User Role: Whether you are a customer or vendor/store owner
  • Store Information (for Vendors): Store name, location, store code, and store image
  • Contact Information: Email addresses and phone numbers for communication and account recovery

2.2 Transaction and Activity Data

  • Transactions: Purchase history, transaction dates, amounts, product information, and quantities
  • Points and Rewards: Points earned, redeemed points, reward redemptions, and reward history
  • Login Activity: Login timestamps and frequency to monitor account security
  • QR Code Scanning: Data generated through QR code scanning transactions

2.3 Location Information

  • Store Location Data: Physical addresses of stores for vendor partners
  • Device Location: If you grant permission, we may collect your device's location to show nearby stores

2.4 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Features accessed, pages viewed, time spent on the platform, and interaction patterns
  • Technical Information: IP addresses, browser type, and connection information

2.5 Communication Data

  • Notifications: We may store records of notifications sent to your email or through the app
  • Support Communications: Any messages you send to our support team

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Service Provision

  • Creating and maintaining your account
  • Processing transactions and managing your points balance
  • Managing rewards and redemptions
  • Displaying your transaction history and account activity

3.2 Communication

  • Sending account notifications and transactional emails
  • Responding to inquiries and customer support requests
  • Notifying you of changes to our policies or services

3.3 Personalization

  • Customizing your experience based on your preferences
  • Recommending products and rewards relevant to you
  • Improving the relevance of store and product information shown to you

3.4 Analytics and Improvement

  • Analyzing usage patterns to improve our platform
  • Understanding which features are most valuable to users
  • Identifying technical issues and optimizing performance

3.5 Security and Fraud Prevention

  • Monitoring for suspicious account activity
  • Preventing fraud and unauthorized access
  • Protecting the security and integrity of our platform

3.6 Legal and Compliance

  • Complying with legal obligations and regulatory requirements
  • Responding to lawful requests from authorities
  • Establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 Information We Do Not Share

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4.2 Information We May Share

  • Vendors and Store Partners: Transaction history and interaction data may be shared with relevant vendors for order fulfillment and transaction processing.
  • Service Providers: We may share information with third-party providers such as cloud hosting and email services.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or government request.
  • Business Transfers: If the company is acquired or merged, your information may be transferred as part of the business assets.

4.3 Aggregated Data

We may share aggregated, anonymized data with partners and the public. This data cannot identify you personally.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Data Storage

Your information is stored on secure servers managed by Supabase, a cloud-based database provider. Data is maintained for as long as your account is active and as required by law or our business operations.

5.2 Security Measures

  • Encryption: Sensitive data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access Control: Only authorized personnel can access your personal information
  • Authentication: Secure login mechanisms including password protection and optional multi-factor authentication
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security assessments and updates to our security protocols

5.3 Limitations

While we take security seriously, no security system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

6. Data Retention

  • Account Information: Retained as long as your account is active
  • Transaction Records: Retained for at least 7 years for compliance and audit purposes
  • User Logs: Retained for security monitoring purposes
  • Inactive Accounts: Marked as inactive but data may be retained per legal requirements

You may request deletion of your account, which will remove most personal information subject to legal retention requirements.
